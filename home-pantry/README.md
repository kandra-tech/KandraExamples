# Home Pantry (Grocy-inspired)

**English** | [Українська](README.uk.md)

What food and household supplies are at home, where, when they spoil, what can be cooked from them and what to buy. Inspired by [Grocy](https://grocy.info/) ("ERP beyond your fridge"), modelled in Kandra's terms; see [NOTICE.md](../NOTICE.md).

## What it shows

**Part A, stock, recipes and shopping**
- Stock as a **balance register by product, location and best-before date** (batches).
- **First-expired-first-out** consumption, a guard against negative stock, transfers and an inventory count.
- Recipes whose ingredients are filled into a Cook document and scaled to the servings.
- Barcode lookup in product selectors, a shopping list that becomes a Purchase, a home **dashboard**.

**Part B, household**
- **Extends the part A configuration** instead of starting a new one.
- Chores and batteries with recurrence, equipment with manuals and warranties, a weekly meal plan feeding part A's shopping needs, daily reminders, more widgets on part A's dashboard.
